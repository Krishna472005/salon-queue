# ALL1 Salon — Project Brief

## The problem
ALL1 is a busy men's grooming salon. On weekends and evenings people stand or sit around for an hour or more
with no idea when their turn is. Some give up and leave, some argue about who arrived first, and the owner
can't see how long people really wait.

## The idea
"Everything. One chair. Zero guessing." Every customer gets a digital token, sees a live estimate on their
phone, and is called by name when a barber is free.

## Who uses it
| User | Device | Login |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | own phone | none (phone number identifies the spot); optional Google sign-in |
| Receptionist | tablet | Google (allow-listed email) |
| Barber (Mahesh, Suresh, Ashlesh) | phone | Google (allow-listed email) |
| Owner | tablet / laptop | Google (allow-listed email) |

## Barbers
Mahesh · Suresh · Ashlesh

## Services (the full menu)
| Service | Time | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Haircut | 60 min | ₹300 |
| Beard trimming | 40 min | ₹400 |
| Facial | 45 min | ₹750 |
| Hair wash | 20 min | ₹150 |
| Massage | 20 min | ₹200 |

A customer may combine up to 4 services in one visit (time and price are added up).

## Must-have features
1. Take a token from your phone: choose services and a barber (or "Any available").
2. Live status page: place in line, estimated wait, "Head over now" alert, full-screen "You're up!".
3. "I'm running late" button: gives a one-time 5-minute extension after being called.
4. Reception board: add walk-ins, call next, mark no-show, cancel.
5. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Finish, take a break.
6. Waiting-room TV: what each barber is doing + next 5 tokens.
7. Owner panel: edit services, choose who is working today, open/close online tokens, daily numbers.
8. Google sign-in for staff with roles; data saved in a real database and synced live.
9. English and Gujarati on every screen.

## Not building
Online payment, booking for future dates, SMS/WhatsApp messages, loyalty points, multiple branches,
ratings/reviews, chatbot.

Fresh "ocean & coral" style: deep navy backgrounds, cobalt and aqua highlights, a warm coral call-to-action,
crisp white cards. Modern, clean, mobile-first. Brand: **ALL1** (ઓલ વન).
